The rush-hour problem

When it gets busy, everything falls apart at once.

Long cashier queues

Guests wait in line twice, once to order and again to pay, for dine-in and takeaway alike.

A kitchen with no clear queue

Order tickets get lost between the floor and the kitchen, so prep slows down right when it matters.

“Which table was that?”

Waiters walk the floor with a tray, hunting for the guest who ordered at the counter.

How it works

From scan to served in four steps.

01

Scan

Every table has its own QR code. Guests see the full menu with photos and prices.

02

Order

Pick variants and add-ons, leave a note for the kitchen. No waiter needed.

03

Pay

Guests pay at the cashier. Once paid, the order goes straight to the kitchen queue.

04

Track

The kitchen works through its queue, guests see live status, and every order is recorded.
