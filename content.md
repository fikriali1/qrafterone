## 4. Pain points
Section headline: When it gets busy, everything falls apart at once.

One restaurant or cafe:
1. **Long cashier queues.** Guests wait in line twice, once to order and again to pay, for dine-in and takeaway alike.
2. **A kitchen with no clear queue.** Order tickets get lost between the floor and the kitchen, so prep slows down right when it matters.
3. **"Which table was that?"** Waiters walk the floor with a tray, hunting for the guest who ordered at the counter.

Franchise or multi-outlet:
1. **No single view of your branches.** You can't see how each outlet is performing without calling around.
2. **Menus drifting across locations.** Prices and items change outlet by outlet, with no central control.
3. **Approvals stuck in WhatsApp.** New menu items are still approved over chats and spreadsheets.

## 5. How it works
Section headline: From scan to served in four steps.
1. **Scan.** Every table has its own QR code. Guests see the full menu with photos and prices.
2. **Order.** Pick variants and add-ons, leave a note for the kitchen. No waiter needed.
3. **Pay.** Guests pay at the cashier. Once paid, the order goes straight to the kitchen queue.
4. **Track.** The kitchen works through its queue, guests see live status, and every order is recorded.

## 6. Features
Section headline: Behind the scenes, you're always in control.
- **Real-time dashboard** (large card): Orders, revenue and average kitchen prep time, updated live. Know your best-selling items without asking your cashier.
- **Kitchen order queue:** Every order lands in one clear queue with table, items and notes, so the kitchen always knows what's next.
- **Tables & QR codes:** Set up indoor, outdoor or any floor area, see which tables are occupied, and print a QR per table.
- **Menu in seconds:** Add items, variants, add-ons and photos, or switch an item off when it sells out, from anywhere.
- **Revenue tracking:** See revenue by day and by outlet, with every order recorded and easy to filter.
- **Multi-outlet view** (franchise): Switch between outlets in one tap, or see every branch in one view.
- **Central menu control** (franchise): Push menu and price changes to all outlets from head office.
- **Menu approvals** (franchise): Outlets propose new items; head office approves them in Qrafter, not in WhatsApp.
